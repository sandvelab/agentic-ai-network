# Logistics

[← Back to overview](../README.md)

## When

Every other Friday, **14:00–15:20**, during the semester.

The biweekly rhythm occasionally shifts around holidays and events. In autumn 2026 the meeting planned for 2 October was moved to **9 October** because of the autumn break. There is **no meeting on 16 October**: the network is instead invited to the TRUST workshop on agentic AI on Monday 19 October (see [below](#related-events)). The series continues on 30 October.

## Where

**Informatikksalen**, 5th floor, [Ole-Johan Dahls hus](https://www.uio.no/om/regelverk/eiendom/leie-lokaler/ole-johan-dahls-hus/) (the Department of Informatics, IFI2), Gaustadalléen 23B, University of Oslo. If a meeting is held in a different room, it will be announced on the email list.

## Physical first, with limited streaming

The series is physical. Open discussion across a room does not work well in hybrid mode, so the discussion part is in-room only.

Introductory presentations may be streamed on Zoom when the presenters agree, so people who cannot attend can still follow them. If you want to take part virtually on a regular basis, the better option is to form a virtual study group (see [About the series](about-the-series.md#growing-as-a-network-of-groups)).

## Staying informed

Announcements, topics and any change of time or place go out on the series' email list: **agentic-ai-network@trust-aicentre.no**. Everyone interested in the series should sign up to it.

## Related events

- **TRUST workshop: Agentic AI — Development, Use & Governance**, Monday 19 October 2026, 14:00–18:00, held as a pre-conference workshop before the first TRUST annual conference (20–21 October). The workshop brings together TRUST researchers and partners, including researchers from outside Oslo, with lightning talks from participants. Everyone in this network is invited.
