# 4 September 2026 — Kickstart

**Time and place:** Friday 4 September 2026, 14:00–15:20, 5th floor, Ole-Johan Dahls hus (IFI)

## Background

The invitation went out through TRUST on 29 August to an initial informal network, with a request to forward it to anyone interested. The starting point was the shared sense that agentic AI is arriving at such speed, and with such broad potential for changing how research is done, that it is hard to keep up individually; that everyone has different experiences and viewpoints; and that the best way to stay updated is personal exchange in a stable group.

## What we did

The meeting was an open discussion of expectations and wishes for the group, with many participants contributing from across the room.

## What we agreed

- Meetings run **biweekly**, Fridays 14:00–15:20.
- The invitation to be part of the group is **completely open**.
- Contributing is welcome but **not required**, and **no prerequisite knowledge** is needed.
- The group should be **consistent**: people who join intend to come regularly, understanding that it will not always work out.
- Discussions are mainly pitched at people with **a fair amount of experience** using agentic AI. The emphasis is on experience sharing for experienced users, not on teaching the basics.
- Coming meetings will include more **presentations**, which gives streaming a better chance to work than the open discussion did.

## Notes

An attempt to connect remote participants via Zoom showed that an open, room-wide discussion is hard to follow virtually. The series therefore stays physical, with streaming limited to presentations.
