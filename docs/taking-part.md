# How to take part and contribute

[← Back to overview](../README.md)

## Joining

Just come. There is no registration, and you are welcome to attend a couple of times to see whether the group suits you. If you decide to stay, we ask that you intend to come regularly. See [why stability matters](about-the-series.md#stability-is-the-point).

To get invitations, sign up to the email list **agentic-ai-network@trust-aicentre.no** (see [Logistics](logistics.md#staying-informed)).

## Contributing

Contributing is welcome and never required. Useful contributions include:

- **A short introduction** (10–20 minutes) to open a topical session: how you have set up agentic AI for a particular kind of work, a system you have built, something that failed and why.
- **A question or challenge** you would like the group to work through.
- **A topic proposal** for a future session.

The most useful introductions tend to be concrete and honest about cost: what it took to set up, what it saves, where it breaks, and what you would do differently.

To propose a contribution, get in touch with Geir Kjetil Sandve (geirksa@ifi.uio.no).

## Starting a new group

If you would like a group at a different time, in a different format (for example fully virtual) or with a particular focus, you are encouraged to start one. New groups run on the same terms (stable, self-organised, regular) and become part of the network, with occasional joint meetings between groups. Get in touch and we will help connect interested people.
