# Agentic AI Network — study groups on agentic AI in research

A TRUST-arranged series of regular study-group meetings where researchers exchange experience on how to use modern agentic AI effectively and appropriately in research and data science.

## What the series is about

Agentic AI — systems that plan a task, carry it out over many steps and check their own work — is arriving fast enough, and with broad enough consequences for how research is done, that it is hard to keep up individually. Everyone has different experiences and different viewpoints, and there is little settled guidance in the literature. The series is built on the belief that the best way to stay on top of it is through sustained, personal exchange of experiences in a group that builds up shared reference points over time.

The subject is process as much as tools: what kinds of tasks can be handed over, how to set them up so that handing over works, which parts of the research process get dramatically faster, and which projects become worth attempting now that were not before.

**The series is completely open to anyone interested.** No prerequisite knowledge is required, and contributing is welcome but never required. It is, however, meant for people who want to be a **consistent part of the group**, coming regularly (as far as possible, of course — not every meeting will work for everyone). It is not a drop-in seminar series: the value comes from a stable group that knows what has been covered before. Discussions are mainly pitched at people who already have a fair amount of hands-on experience with agentic AI; basics are covered elsewhere.

## Veridical data science — agentic AI that reflects reality, reproducibly

We want agentic AI work to be both **reproducible** and to **reflect reality in a stable way**. Veridical data science (VDS) and its PCS framework — Predictability, Computability, Stability — give a vocabulary for exactly that: a result is only worth trusting if it predicts, can be recomputed, and holds up under reasonable alternative choices of data, method and analysis. When much of the analysis is carried out by an agent, those questions become sharper, and some become easier to answer: an agent can track provenance, rerun from a clean slate and systematically vary the choices a human would never have time to vary.

The series will return to this as a recurring thread rather than a separate track.

More: [Veridical data science and agentic AI](docs/veridical-agentic-ai.md)
